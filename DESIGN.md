## Overview

The ITECH Groups Nigeria visual system draws direct inspiration from high-end organic futurism and architectural luxury. Embodying the intersection of African technological sovereignty and global institutional prestige, the design shifts away from conventional dark mode SaaS dashboards into a clean, sun-drenched architectural bento composition: flowing sand-toned concrete contours, floor-to-ceiling glass pavilions, obsidian graphite capsules, and glowing amber highlights.

## Colors

- **Architectural Off-White Canvas (`#f8fafc`):** Clean, airy, museum-grade surface background providing maximum contrast.
- **Deep Obsidian Graphite (`#0f172a` / `#181d27`):** High-density dark capsules, typography, and contrast elements.
- **Warm Golden Amber (`#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24`):** Featured prominently in hero tag badges (e.g. `[FUTURE.]`), milestone nodes, and active investor highlights.
- **Subtle Surface Borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1`):** Precision 1px architectural lines outlining rounded cards and pill buttons.

## Typography

- **Display & Headlines:** Bold, commanding uppercase typography with tight line heights (`leading-[1.05]`) and intentional tracking.
- **Highlight Badges:** High-contrast rounded rectangle badges (e.g., `[FUTURE.]` in bold black text on vibrant amber ground).
- **Rotated Vertical Typography:** Utilized on side capsules for impact metrics (`1M+ COMPLETED IMPACT`).
- **Body Text:** Balanced, readable slate-600 and slate-300 tones with generous line height for fatigue-free reading.

## Layout

- **Asymmetric Bento Composition:**
  - **Left Wing:** Rounded scoop window previewing architectural tech pavilions, floating capsule badge (`SOVEREIGN TECH ↗`), and bold numerical statistics (`7+ SUBSIDIARIES`).
  - **Center Pavilion:** Dominant central card featuring panoramic curved glass architecture, bathed in warm sunset lighting, overlain with massive display typography.
  - **Right Wing:** Vertical stack comprising a dark graphite editorial capsule with pill CTA (`EXPLORE MORE`) and a capsule window with rotated vertical typography.
- **Monochrome Institutional Strip:** High-end horizontal strip of minimalist line-art partner logos below the hero.

## Elevation & Depth

- Elevation is communicated through generous organic border radii (`rounded-[24px]` through `rounded-[36px]`), subtle drop shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`), and floor-to-ceiling glass interior illumination.

## Shapes

- Continuous organic curves inspired by biomorphic desert architecture.
- Full pill radii (`rounded-full`) for all primary interaction buttons, navigation pills, and category filters.

## Components

- **Pill Header Navigation:** Minimalist top navigation bar with uppercase tracking, smooth hover dropdowns, and an authoritative filled `[INVESTORS]` pill button.
- **Bento Stat Cards:** Bold tabular numbers paired with concise uppercase descriptive metadata.
- **Interactive Subsidiary Tabs:** Contrast-rich segmented tabs switching across the 7 operating divisions.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Emphasize expansive architectural space, organic curves, and warm glowing interior illumination.
- Use the amber pill highlight for key focal words (e.g., `[FUTURE.]`).
- Maintain crisp contrast between light bone surfaces and dark obsidian cards.
- Keep all CTA buttons styled as sleek luxury pills (`rounded-full`).

### Don'ts
- Do NOT revert to generic dark-mode cyber grids or neon purple gradients.
- Do NOT use square, sharp-cornered boxes — always maintain the organic 24px-36px radius.
- Do NOT clutter headers with redundant links; keep the 4-item investor-focused hierarchy.
